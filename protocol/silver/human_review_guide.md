# Blinded Human Review Guide

Judge only the displayed body, UTC time, and reply/quote/media flags. Do not open links, parent posts, images, or videos, and do not search for external facts.

Choose one label for each item:

- **ADMISSIBLE**: the displayed text supports at least one real-world event target that can be organized or archived.
- **REJECT**: the displayed text does not support such a target. It may contain no concrete event, use an event only as background, or omit context needed to identify the target.

`REJECT` means that the item is not admitted on the displayed evidence. It does not assert that no event occurred in the world.

Comments, replies, historical events, small events, and personal experiences can be ADMISSIBLE when the event is primary information. Do not reject an item merely because its date, location, participants, or knowledge-base entry are incomplete. Explicit decisions, announcements, plans, and ongoing actions may qualify; wishes, hypotheticals, and conditional discussions alone do not establish an event.

The ambiguity flag and note are optional. Human reviewers do not assign the model-oriented A1/A2/R1/R2/R3 clause codes and need not quote an evidence span.
